Cristina’s Priorities for WRDSB

Student Success
& Wellbeing

Establishing educational excellence through strong literacy and numeracy, age-appropriate learning, physical activity, creativity and balanced use of technology — fostering capable, confident, healthy and resilient students.

Meaningful Parent
Participation

Ensuring that parents genuinely contribute to decisions affecting their children — helping families navigate the path forward and when concerns extend beyond the Board’s authority, carrying those voices forward.

Supporting Educators
to Teach

Removing unnecessary barriers and ensuring safe, well-supported classrooms so educators can use their professional expertise where it matters most — delivering effective instruction with measurable results.

Truth, Trust
& Transparency

Building public confidence through honest two-way dialogue, transparent decision-making, clear accountability and responsible governance — where differing views are heard and personal beliefs are not substituted for fact nor imposed through policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Cristina mean by public education that serves the public?

I believe a publicly funded school board should welcome scrutiny, respect differing views and distinguish evidence from opinion. Students’ learning and wellbeing should remain central, and families and community members should be able to understand and contribute to decisions.

Read the supporting platform priorities

How would Cristina help families raise concerns and get answers?

I would pursue clearer, more reasonable pathways for raising and resolving concerns, alongside stronger two-way communication between families, trustees and the Board. Families should know who makes decisions and where to turn when concerns remain unresolved. When issues fall outside the Board’s authority, I would carry those concerns forward to the province.

Read the supporting platform priorities

How would Cristina ensure community input shapes WRDSB decisions?

I would advocate for meaningful consultation before decisions are made, stronger roles for school councils and the Parent Involvement Committee, and regular community conversations and town halls. Consultation should include follow-up explaining what was heard, what was decided and why.

Read the supporting platform priorities

How would Cristina evaluate student learning and wellbeing?

I would pursue clear, measurable student outcomes and regular evaluation of programs. My priorities include stronger literacy and numeracy, earlier support when students fall behind, and learning that supports capable, confident, healthy and resilient students. That includes purposeful, balanced technology use alongside physical activity, outdoor time, creativity and hands-on learning.

Read the supporting platform priorities

How would Cristina support educators in their classrooms?

I would pursue safer, more orderly classrooms and timely support for students with complex learning, behavioural or developmental needs. I would also advocate for meaningful educator input into classroom-related policy, professional development tied to educational excellence, and professional support that helps educators focus on effective teaching.

Read the supporting platform priorities

How would Cristina make board decisions and spending more transparent?

The public should be able to understand what was decided, why, what evidence supported it, what it cost and whether it worked. I would pursue clearer reporting, accessible board information, measurable goals and regular evaluation, with stronger accountability for public funds and educational results.

Read the supporting platform priorities
